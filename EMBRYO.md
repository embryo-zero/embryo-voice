# Embryo Voice

Embryo Voice is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Coqui TTS](https://github.com/coqui-ai/TTS) (v0.22.0-9-gdbf1a08a, commit dbf1a08a). It does text to speech.

- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
